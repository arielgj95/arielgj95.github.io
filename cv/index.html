Summary Postdoctoral Researcher at the Italian Institute of Technology (IIT), working on multimodal generative learning, room acoustics, and spatial audio. My research spans few-shot room impulse response generation, audio-visual scene modeling, human motion generation, and speaker-independent culture modeling. My publications span ECCV, IEEE RO-MAN, and the International Journal of Social Robotics.

Work

  • 2025.02 - Present

    Genoa, Italy

    Postdoctoral Researcher
    Italian Institute of Technology (IIT)
    Research with the PAVIS team on the European XTREME project, developing learning-based spatial audio and multimodal models for immersive environments.
    • Developing FACT-RIR, a unified pipeline for few-shot room impulse response generation that combines neural audio codecs and conditional flow matching with sparse acoustic observations, source/receiver geometry, and visual evidence; investigating room-level and query-dependent representations and physics-informed conditioning.
    • Built a 3D sound-source localization pipeline mapped onto room point clouds using MVDR, DAS, and MaxRE beamforming.
    • Curating multimodal spatial-audio data and writing a survey on learning-based spatial audio rendering.
  • 2021.11 - 2025.05

    Genoa, Italy / London, UK

    PhD Researcher
    University of Genoa; research collaboration with King's College London
    Developed culture-aware co-speech gesture generation for social robots, combining multimodal generative models, domain generalization, and semantic gesture labeling.
    • Developed rule-based and GAN-based methods for culture-aware co-speech gesture generation on social robots, with publications at AIRO and in the International Journal of Social Robotics.
    • Analyzed cultural cues in multimodal interactions using classical and deep learning models, including MLPs, SVMs, Random Forests, and VQ-VAEs, with publications at AIRO and AHRI at ACII.
    • Developed semantic-similarity algorithms for symbolic and deictic gesture labeling, published at IEEE RO-MAN 2024.
    • Built TED4C-L, a multimodal dataset with 106.45 hours from 764 speakers across four cultural groups, and learned speaker-independent culture embeddings with Fishr regularization and adversarial learning.
    • Designed ALaDiT, a diffusion-transformer model for culture-aware co-speech motion generation, as part of SICAGE, published at ECCV 2026.

Projects

  • FACT-RIR: Few-shot Room Impulse Response Generation (ongoing research)
    A unified pipeline for few-shot room impulse response generation from sparse acoustics, geometry, and visual evidence.
    • Combines neural audio codecs and conditional flow matching in a single latent-space RIR synthesis pipeline.
    • Investigates room-level and query-dependent acoustic representations, decay conditioning, and physics-informed modeling.
