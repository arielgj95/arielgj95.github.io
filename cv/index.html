Summary Postdoctoral researcher with a PhD in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. Develops multimodal generative models for human motion and spatial audio, combining diffusion transformers, representation learning, dataset development, and experimental evaluation in Python and PyTorch.

Work

  • 2025.02 - Present

    Genoa, Italy

    ITALIAN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (IIT) | Postdoctoral Researcher

    Multimodal learning and spatial audio for immersive environments in the European XTREME project.

    • Developing few-shot room impulse response generation from audio and depth, with counterfactual training for reverberation control (ongoing research).
    • Built Eigenmike 64 processing pipelines for beamforming, binaural rendering, and acoustic mapping on 3D room point clouds.
    • Implemented audio/video synchronization and 360-degree acoustic overlays for spatial-audio recordings.
  • 2021.11 - 2025.05

    Genoa, Italy / London, UK

    UNIVERSITY OF GENOA | PhD Researcher

    Research on multimodal human motion generation and domain generalization, including a research collaboration with King's College London.

    • Designed ALaDiT, a diffusion transformer conditioned on speech, text, and cultural representations for 3D gesture generation (SICAGE, ECCV 2026).
    • Built a video-to-3D-motion pipeline and released TED4C-L: 106.45 hours of aligned motion, audio, and text from 764 speakers.
    • Learned cultural representations with Fishr and adversarial domain generalization, using speaker-disjoint training and evaluation.
    • Evaluated motion quality, diversity, and speech alignment through baseline comparisons and controlled ablations; released training and evaluation code.
    • Developed semantic-similarity algorithms for symbolic and deictic gesture labeling (IEEE RO-MAN 2024).
  • 2021.05 - 2021.10

    Italy

    AKKA TECHNOLOGIES | Software Engineer
    • Developed Python backend software for aircraft panels and presented a working demo to stakeholders.
