01 · Motivation

Culture without memorizing the speaker.

Culture-conditioned gesture models are often evaluated with the same speakers in training and testing. That makes it difficult to know whether a model learned shared cultural patterns or simply remembered individual gesturing styles.

SICAGE treats each speaker as a separate domain. Fishr or adversarial regularization keeps cultural representations discriminative while reducing speaker dependence. ALaDiT then combines those embeddings with speech, text, and a short motion seed to synthesize aligned upper-body gestures in real time.

02 · Method

One modular pipeline.

Every component is replaceable: dataset processing, culture encoder, and motion generator.

1

Build TED4C-L

Five-second windows pair 3D upper-body motion with audio, prosody, text embeddings, culture, and speaker identity.

2

Generalize across speakers

Fishr or adversarial learning extracts culture-discriminative audio/text cues while suppressing speaker-specific shortcuts.

3

Generate with ALaDiT

A 50-step diffusion model aligns motion with low-level audio and high-level text/culture context in under 14 ms.

Rebuttal ablations

The gain is not just a label or alignment loss.

FI improves FGD over OneHot (1.63 → 1.03) and NoDG (1.56 → 1.03). Removing ALaDiT alignment also degrades FGD to 1.36. Adding alignment to DSG+/FI improves its FGD from 4.89 to 2.52, but it remains behind ALaDiT/FI.

05 · TED4C-L

A large, speaker-disjoint cultural gesture dataset.

TED4C-L contains 106.45 hours from 764 speakers across India, Italy, Japan, and Turkey. The public release provides numeric motion, audio, and text representations with fixed speaker-independent splits.
